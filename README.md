# Battery Storage Tolling Settlement

Calculate tolling revenue, availability payments, efficiency adjustments, dispatch performance, and penalties.

**Primary buyer:** Battery owners, tolling counterparties, and asset managers. **Evidence:** tolling agreements, capacity commitments, dispatch instructions, telemetry, state of charge, availability, efficiency, market revenue, penalties, invoices, and settlements.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Tolling agreement library
- Battery asset registry
- Capacity commitment
- Dispatch instruction ingestion
- Telemetry validation
- State-of-charge reconciliation
- Round-trip efficiency
- Availability calculation
- Energy throughput calculation
- Market revenue allocation
- Performance penalty audit
- Settlement statement matching
- Counterparty dispute workflow
- Cash reconciliation
- Asset market analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4716>. API: `5716`.
